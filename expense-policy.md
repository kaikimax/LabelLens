# Atlas Meridian Consulting — Travel & Expense Policy

**Version:** 2026.1 · **Effective:** 1 January 2026 · **Owner:** Finance Department
**Applies to:** all employees and contractors of Atlas Meridian Consulting SARL (fictional company, created for testing purposes).

All amounts are in Moroccan dirhams (MAD) unless stated otherwise. Expenses in a foreign currency are converted to MAD at the exchange rate on the date of the expense.

---

## 1. General principles

**1.1 Business purpose.** An expense is reimbursable only if it was incurred for a legitimate business purpose. Each expense must include a short description of that purpose.

**1.2 Reasonableness.** Employees must spend company money as carefully as they would their own. Choosing a more expensive option when a cheaper, equivalent one was available may lead to partial reimbursement, even if no explicit limit in this policy is exceeded.

**1.3 Personal expenses.** Personal expenses are never reimbursable. This includes, among others: personal entertainment, minibar items, in-room movies, laundry on trips shorter than 5 nights, fines, and traffic or parking tickets.

**1.4 Responsibility.** By submitting a report, the employee confirms the expenses are accurate, business-related, and have not been reimbursed from another source.

## 2. Receipts and documentation

**2.1 Receipt required.** An itemized receipt is required for every expense of **100 MAD or more**. Card statements and payment terminal slips alone are not accepted as receipts.

**2.2 Missing receipt.** If a receipt is lost, the employee may submit a signed missing-receipt declaration. No more than **2 missing-receipt declarations** are accepted per calendar quarter, and none is accepted for expenses above **1,000 MAD**.

**2.3 Legibility.** A receipt must clearly show the merchant name, the date, the total amount, and the currency. Unreadable receipts are treated as missing.

**2.4 Submission deadline.** Expenses must be submitted within **60 days** of the expense date. Expenses submitted between 61 and 90 days late require manager approval. Expenses older than **90 days** are not reimbursed.

**2.5 Duplicates.** The same receipt must never be submitted twice. Two expenses with the same merchant, date, and amount are treated as a potential duplicate and are held for review.

## 3. Meals

**3.1 Individual meals while travelling.** When travelling overnight for business, meals are reimbursed up to the following daily limits per person:

| Location | Daily meal limit |
|---|---|
| Morocco — Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier | 400 MAD |
| Morocco — other cities | 300 MAD |
| International — Tier A (Paris, London, Geneva, New York, Dubai, Tokyo) | 1,100 MAD |
| International — other cities | 750 MAD |

The daily limit applies to all meals in a day combined, not to each meal.

**3.2 Meals in your home city.** Meals in the city where the employee is based are not reimbursable, except for business meals under section 3.3.

**3.3 Business meals with clients.** Meals with clients or prospects are reimbursed up to **450 MAD per attendee**, including employees. The receipt or expense description must list the names and companies of all attendees.

**3.4 Team meals.** Internal team meals require prior approval from a manager at Grade M2 or above, and are limited to **250 MAD per attendee**.

**3.5 Alcohol.** Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a business meal with clients (section 3.3), and must not exceed **30% of the meal total**. Alcohol is never reimbursable on individual or team meals.

**3.6 Tips.** Tips are reimbursable up to **10%** of the pre-tip amount.

## 4. Travel

**4.1 Booking.** All flights and train journeys must be booked through the company travel portal. Bookings made outside the portal are reimbursed only up to the price the portal showed for an equivalent journey on the booking date.

**4.2 Air travel class.** Economy class is required for all flights under **6 hours**. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires written approval from a Director (Grade D1 or above) before booking.

**4.3 Trains.** Second class is the standard. First class is allowed for journeys longer than 3 hours.

**4.4 Taxis and ride-hailing.** Taxis and ride-hailing services are reimbursed for business travel. A single ride above **300 MAD** must include an explanation in the description. Late-night rides home after 22:00 following work are reimbursed.

**4.5 Personal vehicle.** Business use of a personal car is reimbursed at **3.00 MAD per kilometre**. Fuel receipts are not accepted for personal vehicles; the mileage rate covers fuel.

**4.6 Car rental.** Rental cars must be economy or compact category. Fuel for rental cars is reimbursable with a receipt. Rental insurance offered at the counter must not be purchased, because the company's policy already covers it.

**4.7 Parking and tolls.** Parking and highway tolls are reimbursable for business journeys. Airport parking is limited to **100 MAD per day**.

## 5. Lodging

**5.1 Nightly limits.** Hotel costs (room rate plus taxes, excluding meals) are reimbursed up to:

| Location | Nightly limit |
|---|---|
| Morocco — Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier | 1,200 MAD |
| Morocco — other cities | 900 MAD |
| International — Tier A | 2,800 MAD |
| International — other cities | 1,800 MAD |

**5.2 Exceptions.** A rate above the limit may be accepted during major events or trade fairs if the employee documents that no compliant option was available within reasonable distance of the business location.

**5.3 Extras.** Hotel internet access for business use is reimbursable. Minibar, room service beyond the meal limits, spa, and gym fees are not.

## 6. Other expenses

**6.1 Mobile and internet.** A monthly phone and internet allowance of up to **200 MAD** is reimbursable with an invoice, unless the employee has a company phone line.

**6.2 Software and subscriptions.** Software, SaaS subscriptions, and online services must be purchased by IT. Individual purchases are not reimbursed unless pre-approved by IT in writing.

**6.3 Training and books.** Professional books are reimbursable up to **500 MAD per quarter**. Training courses and conferences require prior manager approval.

**6.4 Gifts.** Gifts to clients are limited to **500 MAD per recipient per year** and must not be cash or cash equivalents (gift cards included). Gifts to public officials are prohibited.

**6.5 Weekend and holiday expenses.** Expenses dated on a weekend or public holiday require a description explaining the business reason, unless they are part of a trip that spans that day.

## 7. Approval thresholds

**7.1 Report approval.** All reports require approval by the employee's direct manager.

**7.2 High-value expenses.** Any single expense above **5,000 MAD** additionally requires approval by a Director (Grade D1 or above).

**7.3 Self-approval.** No employee may approve their own expenses or expenses of a more senior employee.

## 8. Employee grades

For the purposes of this policy, employee grades are:

| Grade | Title |
|---|---|
| E1–E3 | Consultant to Senior Consultant |
| M1–M2 | Manager to Senior Manager |
| D1 | Director |

Unless a section says otherwise, limits apply equally to all grades.

## 9. Non-compliance

Expenses that break this policy are rejected or reimbursed only up to the compliant amount. Repeated or deliberate breaches may lead to disciplinary action. Questions about this policy should be sent to Finance before the expense is incurred.
